Bibliography

References used in the research for this project, organized by source type.

Books

Academic Books & Textbooks

Foundational textbooks and academic works consulted in the research and preparation of this project.

Academic Book
Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control

CRAIG, John J. Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control. 3rd ed. Upper Saddle River: Pearson Prentice Hall, 2005. 408 p. ISBN 978-0201543612.

The definitive undergraduate textbook on robotics kinematics, dynamics, and trajectory planning. Used as the primary technical reference for the mechanics and types sections of this project.

Academic Book
Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach

RUSSELL, Stuart; NORVIG, Peter. Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach. 4th ed. Hoboken: Pearson, 2020. 1132 p. ISBN 978-0134610993.

The most widely adopted AI textbook worldwide. Chapters on robotics, planning, and machine learning provided the conceptual framework for our coverage of AI-driven robots and autonomous systems.

Reference Work
Springer Handbook of Robotics

SICILIANO, Bruno; KHATIB, Oussama (Ed.). Springer Handbook of Robotics. 2nd ed. Berlin/Heidelberg: Springer-Verlag, 2016. 2222 p. ISBN 978-3319325507.

A comprehensive encyclopedic reference covering all major areas of robotics, from manipulation and locomotion to human-robot interaction, medical robotics, and field robotics.

Academic Book
Introduction to Robotics: Analysis, Control, Applications

NIKU, Saeed B. Introduction to Robotics: Analysis, Control, Applications. 2nd ed. Hoboken: Wiley, 2010. 460 p. ISBN 978-0470604465.

An accessible introduction to robot analysis and design, covering kinematics, dynamics, control systems, and practical applications with worked examples useful for students at all levels.

Literature & Journals

Fiction, Periodicals & Online Sources

Foundational science fiction works that shaped our cultural understanding of robots, plus peer-reviewed journals and official agency publications.

Science Fiction
I, Robot

ASIMOV, Isaac. I, Robot. New York: Doubleday, 1950. 253 p. ISBN 978-0385333481.

The classic short story collection that introduced the famous Three Laws of Robotics. Asimov foresaw many ethical dilemmas now faced by AI researchers. Essential reading for understanding the cultural and philosophical roots of modern robotics discourse.

Scientific Journal
IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine

IEEE ROBOTICS AND AUTOMATION SOCIETY. IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine. Piscataway: IEEE, 2024. Quarterly periodical. Available at: <https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ram>. Accessed: Sep. 10, 2026.

Leading peer-reviewed publication bridging academic research and industrial applications. Multiple 2023-2024 issues consulted for current trends in cobots, medical robotics, and autonomous systems.

Access Journal
Official Agency
NASA Mars 2020 Mission: Perseverance Rover

NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration). Mars 2020 Mission: Perseverance Rover Overview. Washington, D.C.: NASA/JPL-Caltech, 2024. Available at: <https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/>. Accessed: Sep. 11, 2026.

Official mission documentation, scientific findings, and technical specifications for the Perseverance rover, including the Ingenuity helicopter. Primary source for the space exploration section of this project.

Access Mission Page
Industry Report
World Robotics Report 2024

INTERNATIONAL FEDERATION OF ROBOTICS (IFR). World Robotics Report 2024. Frankfurt: IFR, 2024. Available at: <https://ifr.org/worldrobotics/>. Accessed: Sep. 12, 2026.

The authoritative annual statistical report on global industrial and service robot installations, trends, and forecasts. Used for all statistical data presented in the industry and applications sections of this project.

Access Report
Transparency

About Our Sources

Research Methodology: Following the guidelines of the ETEC Open Doors academic fair, all books, articles, journals, and websites consulted in the preparation of this project are listed above, respecting the intellectual property rights of their respective authors and publishers.

References are formatted following APA 7th Edition conventions. All online sources were verified as accessible in September 2026. Where possible, we linked directly to official publisher or agency pages.

Academic Integrity: This project was created as a non-profit academic work for educational purposes. All content is original student writing based on the referenced sources. Images used are either original, used under Creative Commons license, or from public domain sources, with credits noted in the Credits page.

No generative AI was used to produce the textual content of this project. All research, writing, and web development was performed by the student team under the supervision of their teachers.

GOOD TO KNOW

The IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is the world largest technical professional organization, with over 400,000 members in 160 countries. The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, founded in 1984, publishes the most cited journals in the robotics field and organizes ICRA, the premier international robotics conference held annually since 1984.
